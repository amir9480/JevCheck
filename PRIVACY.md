# Jev Check privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-14

Jev Check is an independent browser extension. It has no server, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer does not receive, store or process any of your data.

## What stays on your device

Question sets, reference documents, evaluation history, settings and your API keys are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB and extension storage). Keys are encrypted at rest. Uninstalling the extension deletes this data.

## What is sent, and to whom

When you press **Evaluate**, Jev Check sends the text of the current page (or your selection), your question set and its context, and any documents attached to that set to **the one AI provider you selected**, authenticated with your own API key. Nothing is sent before you press Evaluate, and nothing is sent to any other party. The provider's privacy policy and terms govern how it handles that data. Do not evaluate pages or documents you may not share or that contain sensitive personal data.

## Permissions

Jev Check reads a page only after you click its toolbar icon (or use its context-menu entry) on that tab. Access to a provider's host is requested only when you add a key for it.
